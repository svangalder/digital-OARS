Development is Not Linear

Research shows that children develop counting skills in varied ways:

  • Concurrent skills: Students often work on multiple levels simultaneously (e.g., counting to 20 while learning to count backward)
  • Variable pacing: Different rates of progress are expected and developmentally normal
  • Context-dependent: Success in one setting (organized objects) may precede success in another (mixed piles)
