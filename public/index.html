Children develop counting skills in varied, overlapping ways.

  • Variable pacing: Different rates are expected and normal

This is an assessment tool. Each save records one counting attempt you observed.

How to use this tool

A short guide to the workflow and the screens.

This is an assessment tool. Each save records one counting attempt you observed.
1
Add students

Type each name and tap "+ Add" — or tap "+ Add several at once" to paste a list (one per line). If two students share a first name, add a last initial (e.g. "Ben S.").

2
Edit or delete a student

Tap the ⋯ menu on any student card to rename or delete them.

3
Record observations

Tap a student to open their card. Pick the collection size first (it's required), then for each of the three counting principles tap Yes (correct) or one of the error chips. Save and move to the next student.

The three principles: Number Word Sequence — saying numbers in order. One-to-One Correspondence — one number word per object. Cardinality — knowing the last number said is the total.

4
Use "More details" only when you want to

Most observations only need size + principles. Tap More details if you want to record arrangement (lined up / scattered) or who set up the objects. These extras help the Groups tab find sharper patterns over time.

5
Group & teach

The Groups tab suggests small groups of students who'd benefit from the same kind of practice, based on each student's most recent observation. To remove a student from a group, just record a new observation showing the skill is now correct — groups update automatically.

6
Switch the home view

Tap the Roster / Class View toggle on the home screen to switch between per-student cards and a compact whole-class summary.

How your data is protected
What is stored

Student first names and observation data only. No last names, no photos, no account information, no email.

All data stays on this device, encrypted with your PIN. Nothing is sent to any server.
Encryption

Your data is encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your PIN (PBKDF2, 250,000 iterations). Without the PIN, the data on disk cannot be read.

Backups

Because your PIN is the only key, we can't reset it, and clearing browser data will erase your classroom. Tap Export weekly — it saves a CSV backup that you control.

A weekly export is the simple safety net. The app will gently remind you after 7 days.
Speech-to-text

If you use the dictation button on a note, your browser may send audio to an external server (e.g. Google's speech service on Chrome). Avoid dictating last names or identifying details.
