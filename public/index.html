Development is not linear
Research shows children develop counting skills in varied, overlapping ways.
  • Concurrent skills: Students often work on multiple levels simultaneously
  • Variable pacing: Different rates are expected and developmentally normal
  • Context-dependent: Success with organized objects may precede success with mixed piles
Clements, D. H., & Sarama, J. (2017/2019). Learning and teaching with learning trajectories [LT]². Marsico Institute, Morgridge College of Education, University of Denver.
