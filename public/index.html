Welcome

Glad you're here. Here's how this tool works in three steps.

1
Add your students

Type each student's first name into the box at the top of the home screen — or tap "Add several at once" to paste a whole list. First names only.

2
Observe during counting activities

Tap a student to open their card. Tap the size of the collection, then tap "Yes" or one of the error chips for each principle you focused on. Save and move on.

3
See patterns and group students

The home screen shows each student at a glance. The Groups tab suggests small-group practice based on what you've observed.

Tip: You can revisit this guide anytime — tap ? How to use at the top of the home screen.
How to use this tool

A short guide to the workflow and the screens.

1
Add students

Type each name and tap "+ Add" — or tap "+ Add several at once" to paste a list (one per line).

2
Record observations

Tap a student to open their card. Pick the collection size, then for each of the three counting principles tap Yes (correct) or one of the error chips. Save and move to the next student.

The three principles: Number Word Sequence — saying numbers in order. One-to-One Correspondence — one number word per object. Cardinality — knowing the last number said is the total.

3
Use "More details" only when you want to

Most observations only need size + principles. Tap More details if you want to record arrangement (lined up / scattered), who set up the objects, or describe an unusual error. These extras help the Groups tab find sharper patterns over time.

4
Read the home screen

Each compact card shows the student name, last-seen date, and a quick three-dot summary. Tap the chevron to expand the full tracks and trajectory.

5
Group & teach

The Groups tab suggests small groups of students who'd benefit from the same kind of practice, based on the errors you've observed.

How your data is protected
What is stored

Student first names and observation data only. No last names, no photos, no account information, no email.

All data stays on this device, encrypted with your PIN. Nothing is sent to any server.
Encryption

Your data is encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your PIN (PBKDF2, 250,000 iterations). Without the PIN, the data on disk cannot be read.

Backups

Because your PIN is the only key, we can't reset it, and clearing browser data will erase your classroom. Tap Export weekly — it saves a CSV backup that you control.

A weekly export is the simple safety net. The app will gently remind you after 7 days.
Speech-to-text

If you use the dictation button on a note, your browser may send audio to an external server (e.g. Google's speech service on Chrome). Avoid dictating last names or identifying details.
