Development is not linear
Children develop counting skills in varied, overlapping ways.
  • Concurrent skills: Students often work on multiple levels simultaneously
  • Variable pacing: Different rates are expected and normal
  • Context-dependent: Success with organized objects may precede mixed piles
Clements, D. H., & Sarama, J. (2017/2019). Learning and teaching with learning trajectories [LT]². Marsico Institute, Morgridge College of Education, University of Denver.
Welcome to your classroom
Here's how this tool works
👤
Add your students
Type a first name into the field at the top and tap + Add. Each name gets its own card on the home screen.
✏️
Tap a card to observe
Open any student card during a counting activity. Record collection size, mark each principle Correct or Errors, then save.
👥
See who needs support
After a few observations, tap Groups in the top bar. The app will suggest which students to pull for small-group work — and what to try.
📈
Explore the trajectory
Tap Trajectory to see the 7 developmental levels from Reciter to Skip Counter. Each card on the home screen shows where a student currently sits.

Your Data & Privacy
What is stored

Roster and observations stay on this device, encrypted with a key derived from your PIN (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM). Nothing is sent to a server.

✓ Without the correct PIN, the data on disk is unreadable — even with full access to browser storage or developer tools.
What is NOT stored

Last names, ages, birthdates, photos, or any identifying info beyond a first name. Your PIN is never stored — only a verifier derived from it. The encryption key lives in memory only and is cleared the moment you sign out.

Voice dictation caveat

If you enable the microphone for note dictation, your browser's built-in speech recognition typically sends the audio to a third-party service (e.g. Google's servers on Chrome) for transcription. The transcript stays on this device, but the audio does not. Voice dictation is off by default and requires a per-session opt-in.

Backup / export caveat

CSV backups are not encrypted. Once exported, the file lives outside this app's protection — anyone with access to your downloads folder can read it. Treat backups carefully.

Where your data lives

Browser localStorage on this device — stays until you clear browser storage.

⚠ Clearing browser data or switching devices erases records. Download backups regularly. If you forget your PIN, the encrypted data cannot be recovered.
How to delete

Sign out and clear site data in your browser settings.

Use voice dictation?

The mic uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. On most browsers (including Chrome), the audio is sent to a third-party speech service to be transcribed — it does not stay entirely on this device.

⚠ Avoid saying student last names, identifying details, or anything you wouldn't want a third party to hear. The transcript itself stays on this device, but the audio doesn't.
